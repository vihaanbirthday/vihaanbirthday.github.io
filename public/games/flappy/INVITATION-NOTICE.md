# Invitation distribution

Source: https://github.com/nebez/floppybird

Revision: efd2c7f740299688e524e57bfa3f635c9b857f82

Invitation adaptation, September 2026: local-only assets, telemetry removed, silent play, touch and responsive layout fixes. Original gameplay retained.

Tap Tap Tap: uses pointer events to avoid duplicate touch/click activation. Audio files are omitted.

Hogwarts Flyer (formerly Safari Flyer): uses Floppy Bird game logic under Apache-2.0; all original graphic and sound assets are omitted. CSS graphics and text-based score displays are original invitation adaptations. jQuery and jQuery Transit retain their upstream notices.

October 2026: the final result screen adds a guest-initiated score-sharing button. It sends result data to the parent invitation, which generates a local PNG and offers a device share sheet or download plus the configured WhatsApp chat. Game frames retain their original sandbox privileges.

October 2026 motion update: display-synchronized animation frames and elapsed-time flight integration retain the original 60 Hz flight curve. The bird’s angle eases toward its velocity-driven direction to smooth the nose-down to nose-up turn after a flap.

October 2026 Hogwarts Flyer update: original SVG fan illustrations replace the bird with Harry, Ron or Hermione on a broomstick. Character selection is required before takeoff and available after every flight. Moonlit castle scenery and enchanted stone towers replace the jungle design. This is an unofficial Harry Potter fan game; no official film, book, game, graphic or sound assets are distributed. Harry Potter characters and settings belong to their respective rights holders.
