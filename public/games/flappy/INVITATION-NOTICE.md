# Invitation distribution

Revision: efd2c7f740299688e524e57bfa3f635c9b857f82

Invitation adaptation, September 2026: local-only assets, telemetry removed, silent play, touch and responsive layout fixes. Original gameplay retained.

Tap Tap Tap: uses pointer events to avoid duplicate touch/click activation. Audio files are omitted.

Broomstick Flyer (formerly Safari Flyer): uses Floppy Bird game logic under Apache-2.0; all original graphic and sound assets are omitted. CSS graphics and text-based score displays are original invitation adaptations. jQuery and jQuery Transit retain their upstream notices.

October 2026: the final result screen adds a guest-initiated score-sharing button. It sends result data to the parent invitation, which generates a local PNG and offers a device share sheet or download plus the configured WhatsApp chat. Game frames retain their original sandbox privileges.

October 2026 motion update: display-synchronized animation frames and elapsed-time flight integration retain the original 60 Hz flight curve. The bird’s angle eases toward its velocity-driven direction to smooth the nose-down to nose-up turn after a flap.

October 2026 Broomstick Flyer update: original SVG illustrations replace the bird with Nova the moon scholar, Pip the wind apprentice or Lumi the lantern keeper on a broomstick. Character selection is required before takeoff and available after every flight. Original moonlit lakeside academy scenery and enchanted stone towers replace the jungle design. Character silhouettes, outfits, broomsticks and the lakeside academy are created for this invitation; no franchise characters, branding, film, book, game, graphic or sound assets are distributed.

October 2026 academy artwork update: three original human apprentices wear flowing charcoal robes, unstriped neck wraps and individually designed pointed hats. The hand-drawn lakeside campus combines slate towers, lantern cloisters and a copper observatory in an original layout. Character names, portraits and setting are original; no franchise names, logos, insignia, character likenesses, music or copied scenery are included.
