Credits & privacy

Your visit

This invitation has no analytics, advertising, guest accounts, database or outbound API requests. Artwork, fonts and games are hosted locally. Games keep their scores and internal data only in memory, with no cookies or persistent browser storage. Your hosting provider may maintain standard access logs.

RSVP details are used only to prepare a WhatsApp message. You review and send it in WhatsApp. Opening WhatsApp or Google Maps takes you to a third-party service governed by its own privacy policy.

Open-source games

Each game runs in a sandboxed frame with outbound network connections blocked. Scores are not sent to a server. All four games let you choose to share a final score image with Madhav through your device’s share sheet or download it and attach it in WhatsApp. The invitation prepares this image locally; you choose the recipient and send it yourself.

  • Tap Tap Tap — Mahdi Al-Farra. MIT license. Download modified source.
  • Safari Flyer (Floppy Bird adaptation) — original game code by Nebez Briefkani under Apache-2.0. Download modified source. New CSS artwork and text scores replace the original game images. No original Flappy Bird images or sounds are distributed.
  • Hextris — Logan Engstrom, Garrett Finucane, Noah Moroze, and Michael Yang. GPL-3.0-or-later license. Download complete modified source.
  • 0h h1 — Q42, created by Martin Kool. MIT license. Download complete modified source.

Invitation adaptations (September 2026): removed advertising, analytics, social promotions, and external scripts; replaced persistent storage with session memory; applied restrictive content policies; embedded fonts; replaced Hextris function deserialization with data-only JSON; replaced the 0h h1 dynamic script loader with static local scripts. Tap Tap Tap: analytics and audio removed; October 2026 birthday edition adds eight designed levels, hearts, streak multipliers, gold bonus stars, fair target placement, retry controls, local result-image sharing and a redesigned interface. Safari Flyer: original CSS art, responsive play area, no audio or telemetry, local libraries and unified pointer controls. The original games and their licenses remain credited above. Legacy touch listeners are adapted for modern passive-event defaults. All original license notices are retained.
